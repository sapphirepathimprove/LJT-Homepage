**Project Title:** Mathematical Simulation of Ecosystem Structure and Material Circulation

**Award:** National Third Prize, 18th "Challenge Cup" National College Students Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition

**Core Innovation:** Invented mathematical simulation method for ecosystem structure and material circulation

**Research Background:** Ecosystems are complex adaptive systems characterized by nonlinear interactions, multi-scale feedback loops, and emergent behaviors. Understanding the material circulation patterns within these systems is crucial for ecological sustainability and environmental management.

**Methodology:**
- Developed mathematical framework for modeling ecosystem structure based on complex network theory
- Constructed material circulation simulation algorithm incorporating multi-scale dynamics
- Integrated agent-based modeling to simulate emergent ecological phenomena
- Validated simulation results against real-world ecosystem data

**Significance:** This work provides a quantitative tool for understanding ecosystem material circulation, with potential applications in environmental monitoring, ecological restoration, and sustainable resource management.
