**Project Title:** Spatial Information Management Method and System for China's National Geographic Conditions Census

**Award:** National Second Prize, 17th "Challenge Cup" National College Students Extracurricular Academic and Scientific Works Competition

**Core Innovation:** Invented spatial information management method and system for geographic conditions census data

**Research Background:** China's national geographic conditions census involves massive, multi-source, multi-temporal spatial data. Effective management and utilization of this data requires advanced spatial information systems.

**Methodology:**
- Constructed unified spatiotemporal framework integrating multi-scale geographic entities (points, lines, polygons, networks, terrains)
- Developed dynamic update mechanisms for natural geographic elements (terrain, landforms, water systems) and human geographic elements
- Implemented metadata management module supporting four-dimensional data retrieval and visualization
- Designed system architecture for efficient storage, query, and analysis of census data

**Significance:** This work provides technical support for China's national geographic conditions monitoring, urban planning, and sustainable development decision-making.

**Patent Status:** Pending

**Collaborators:** Zhenfeng Shao, Xiao Huang, Deren Li (School of Earth Exploration Science and Technology, Jilin University)
